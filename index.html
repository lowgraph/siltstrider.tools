About Silt Strider

Silt Strider is an unofficial fan project. It is neither directly nor indirectly affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Bethesda Softworks, Bethesda Game Studios, or ZeniMax Media.

It exists as a free reference and planning aid for players of The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind. It is not intended to infringe upon the copyrights of Bethesda Game Studios or its parent and affiliate companies, and reproduces no game assets, art, audio, or text.

Races, classes, birthsigns, skills and gear are checked against data read straight from the game files: Morrowind, Tribunal and Bloodmoon, and for Tamriel Rebuilt, Tamriel_Data and TR_Mainland (26.08 Poison Song). Restrictions and objectives are human curated.

Changelog

What changed on Silt Strider, newest first.

September 19, 2026

  • Character Builder workspace alignment: Custom Class Builder spans directly above Character Configuration, and Premade Builds Catalog spans directly above Character Sheet, framing the desktop layout into two balanced, column-aligned work areas.
  • Enclosed the Character Builder inside the game's authentic 6-pixel ornate parchment window frame with deep inset shading, giving it the same master window treatment as Challenge Runs.
  • Desktop panel spacing: reduced the gap between the Configurator and Character Sheet to 20 pixels, bringing the planner into a tighter, cohesive side-by-side workstation without losing breathing room.
  • Clean skill slots: removed redundant slot index numbers from Major and Minor skill slots, giving dropdown selectors full horizontal space while preserving accessible screen-reader labels.
  • Local Characters saved build manager moved into its own standalone collapsible plaque below the Configurator, styled with a dark bronze header banner, gold chevrons, and etched borders, with Copy Build Link positioned directly below it.
  • Mobile ergonomics: removed the sticky mobile vitals bar so small screens have full, unobstructed height for picking skills and attributes without persistent banners taking up space. Vitals remain cleanly accessible on the Character Sheet tab.
  • Mobile-first Gear Advisor: checkboxes wrap neatly in their own line, and Optimize Gear expands to a full-width action button on mobile screens before settling into an inline control on desktop.
  • Specialization and Favored Attribute dropdowns: removed inline (+5 skills) and (+10) from the option choices so names like Intelligence and Personality never clip behind the dropdown arrow. The info button now sits locked horizontally beside each field title.
  • Character sheet cleanup: removed the math formula breakdown details accordion, keeping the sheet strictly focused on in-game Morrowind stats, attributes, and starting spells.

September 18, 2026

  • Next.js and React architecture: Silt Strider runs on Next.js 16 with React 19, static export, and on-demand game data loading for Vanilla, Tamriel Rebuilt, and ARCE profiles.
  • Rebuilt the Character Planner in React: interactive Configurator, live Character Sheet, Premade Builds catalogue browser, and decoupled Gear Advisor, preserving URL permalinks and local saves.
  • Art direction overhaul and 3-tier frame hierarchy: eliminated line tangencies across the site. The 6-pixel ornate scrollwork frame is reserved for primary panels, 2-pixel etched grooves for plaques and banners, and 4-pixel bevels exclusively for buttons and dropdowns.
  • High-contrast gold focus outlines appear on keyboard navigation across all buttons, dropdowns, and save inputs, and muted text contrast meets WCAG AA standards.
  • Cross-tool calculator integration: Enchanting, Spellmaking, Alchemy, and Travel calculators feature live character HUD banners reflecting Intelligence, Willpower, Luck, skills, and fatigue in real-time barter, cast chance, and brewing numbers.
  • Tactile Armor Rating badges: equipment recommendations display Armor Rating as distinct burnished bronze stat badges with monospace numerals and gold borders.
  • Kit plaques: Early Game and Endgame equipment recommendations are styled as rich collapsible CRPG plaques with Pelagiad serif headings and hover highlights.
  • All seventeen Miscellaneous Skills are listed alphabetically on the character sheet under Miscellaneous Skills, matching the game.
  • Data pipeline and acquisition engine: normalized game-data extraction across Vanilla, Tamriel Rebuilt 26.08, and ARCE 4.1, evaluating merchant stock ownership, pro-rata pricing for worn gear, repairable broken items, and leveled danger budgets based on Samarys Ancestral Tomb.

September 17, 2026

  • Enchanting calculator: pick effects, a soul, and a named enchanter. Charge, gold, and barter follow OpenMW. Constant Effect needs a soul of 400 or more. Tamriel Rebuilt adds extra effects and mainland enchanters when that world is on. Search an enchanter by name if the list is long.
  • Spellmaking calculator: magicka cost and gold follow OpenMW player spellmaking, with your six school skills, Willpower and Luck for cast chance, and named spellmakers for the price you would pay. Tamriel Rebuilt effects appear when that world is on. The same effect cannot be added twice, except Attribute and Skill variants such as Fortify Strength beside Fortify Intelligence.
  • Alchemy calculator: named mortar, alembic, calcinator and retort (no Secretmaster tools), four ingredient slots, and brew numbers from OpenMW. Vanilla ingredients are always there; Tamriel Rebuilt ingredients join the lists when that world is on. Search by name or by effect. The same ingredient cannot occupy two slots.
  • Travel optimizer: fewest hops by silt strider, boat, Guild Guide, and river strider. No propylons and no walking. Vanilla stays on Vvardenfell and Solstheim; Tamriel Rebuilt stops and river striders appear when that world is on.

September 15, 2026

  • Every card on a challenge run has its own randomize and lock buttons, and Randomize all rerolls the cards that aren't locked. The What to randomize ticks, Randomize character and the challenge-run character sheet are gone.
  • New Major skills and Minor skills cards sit beside Restrictions. They show the rolled class's skills, and roll on their own only while the class is Custom or not rolled yet: a skills roll makes a custom class with a random specialization and favored attributes. Class rolls can land on Custom too.
  • Copy permalink copies a link that reopens the same run, and Send to Build Optimizer opens the rolled character in the custom builder.
  • The number of objectives and restrictions, the difficulty bands and the full lists moved to Advanced settings.
  • Restrictions that repeat or undercut each other no longer roll together, such as a level 10 and a level 20 cap, No magic and No spending Magicka, or Marksman only and No ranged weapons.
  • New restrictions: Ironman with no quicksaving, only level one weapon skill, no jumping, no spending Magicka, no activated birthsign or racial powers, and a level 10 cap. Removed No companions, No killing named NPCs, No exploits or glitches, the Calm or Frenzy, water-taunting, Restore Health kiting and Acrobatics cheese bans, The Atronach and no potions, No sleeping to recover magicka, No skill books, and no ingredients from named town containers.
  • The Strength and Intelligence caps and the level 20 cap are Hard instead of Grind.
  • Faction major objectives name each faction's top rank, such as Master of the Fighters Guild, Arch-Mage of the Mages Guild or Patriarch of the Tribunal Temple, and the three guilds are separate objectives. Dropped becoming Hortator of all three Houses and completing every quest in one town.
  • The Tamriel Rebuilt guild, Legion, Temple and Imperial Cult major objectives are now to complete all their quests in five mainland cities. Dropped the Telvanni Isles questline.
  • Removed eleven minor objectives, among them leaving skill books unread, talking to every named NPC in Pelagiad and finding your way out of Vivec's sewers.
  • Starting spells lists what a new character begins with: spells from the race and the birthsign, and the starter spells the game gives for strong enough magic skills.
  • Argonian Water Breathing and Khajiit Eye of Night are listed as spells, not a constant effect and a power, and so are The Lord's Blood of the North and The Serpent's Star-Curse.
  • Starting sheet: skills sit in an even grid, and Health, Fatigue and Magicka share a row.
  • The custom builder's fields sit in two columns.
  • Optimize gear no longer shows a “Full kit by slot” heading.
  • Removed Copy this loadout, Save build and build codes. The address bar keeps a link to the current sheet, and Copy link copies it.
  • About and Changelog open from the links at the bottom of the page. About has separate Corrections and Support sections, with Ko-fi and PayPal, and says restrictions and objectives are curated by hand.
  • Fixed the navigation overlapping the site name in desktop windows narrower than 1200 pixels.
  • The page frame and cards have the game's rough parchment window border, and buttons and dropdowns look like its Name, Race, Class and Sign buttons, drawn pixel for pixel in the colours of its character sheet.
  • The starting sheet shows Health, Magicka and Fatigue as red, blue and green bars, like the game's character sheet.
  • The gear optimizer knows beast races can't wear boots, shoes or helmets that cover the whole head: Argonians, Khajiit and ARCE's beast races get open helmets and no footwear.
  • Every armor pick in the gear tables shows its armor rating.
  • Clearer gear locations: the Bonemold Boots are in Arobar Manor's bedrooms, the Glass Dagger comes from Suran's pawnbroker, and Telvon Llethan's smithy is in Vivec's Hlaalu Canton.
  • Removed the Naked start restriction, which was too easy, and the vague Ghostfence walk objective. No alteration and No lockpicking no longer roll together, and neither do finding Fargoth's hiding place and taking nothing in Seyda Neen.
  • Minor objectives no longer carry a “flavor — no hard checkbox” label.
  • Early-game gear can be stolen at any value when the theft is easy; the 500-gold limit now only covers buying. Medium armor's early kit takes Dreugh and Orcish pieces from Meldor's shop in Balmora, Ralds Oril's in Suran and a crate in Creeper's house in Caldera.
  • The Build Optimizer has two early-game toggles: Steal early gear, on by default, and Endgame gear early, which adds endgame pieces you can steal as easily as Mentor's Ring, such as Stendarr's Hammer from Mournhold's Museum of Artifacts or, in Tamriel Rebuilt, Boots of the Savior's Hide from a shop in Helnim. With Steal off, each row falls back to gear you can buy, repair or pick up.
  • Early gear starts with the closest source in or around Seyda Neen, Pelagiad, Balmora, Caldera, Ald'ruhn, Vivec, Suran and Ebonheart (and Old Ebonheart with Tamriel Rebuilt), even if it costs more, and an “or” row adds a stronger piece from farther away. Buying and repairing in one place beats running between two, and a repair elsewhere names where. Shield builds get one-handed weapons, and Arrille's Tradehouse in Seyda Neen now counts.
