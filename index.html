Choose a tool

New to Morrowind? Start here: pick Challenge Runs to roll a fun set of limits, or Build Optimizer to see starting skills and a kit. Restrictions are extra rules you choose for a harder playthrough. ARCE (All Races and Classes Enabled) appears after you enable Tamriel Rebuilt.

About Silt Strider

Silt Strider is an unofficial fan project. It is neither directly nor indirectly affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Bethesda Softworks, Bethesda Game Studios, or ZeniMax Media.

It exists as a free reference and planning aid for players of The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind. It is not intended to infringe upon the copyrights of Bethesda Game Studios or its parent and affiliate companies, and reproduces no game assets, art, audio, or text.

Races, classes, birthsigns, skills and gear are checked against data read straight from the game files: Morrowind, Tribunal and Bloodmoon, and for Tamriel Rebuilt, Tamriel_Data and TR_Mainland (26.08 Poison Song). Restrictions and objectives are human curated.

Changelog

What changed on Silt Strider, newest first.

September 15, 2026

  • Every card on a challenge run has its own randomize and lock buttons, and Randomize all rerolls the cards that aren't locked. The What to randomize ticks, Randomize character and the challenge-run character sheet are gone.
  • New Major skills and Minor skills cards sit beside Restrictions. They show the rolled class's skills, and roll on their own only while the class is Custom or not rolled yet: a skills roll makes a custom class with a random specialization and favored attributes. Class rolls can land on Custom too.
  • Copy permalink copies a link that reopens the same run, and Send to Build Optimizer opens the rolled character in the custom builder.
  • The number of objectives and restrictions, the difficulty bands and the full lists moved to Advanced settings.
  • Restrictions that repeat or undercut each other no longer roll together, such as a level 10 and a level 20 cap, No magic and No spending Magicka, or Marksman only and No ranged weapons.
  • New restrictions: Ironman with no quicksaving, only level one weapon skill, no jumping, no spending Magicka, no activated birthsign or racial powers, and a level 10 cap. Removed No companions, No killing named NPCs, No exploits or glitches, the Calm or Frenzy, water-taunting, Restore Health kiting and Acrobatics cheese bans, The Atronach and no potions, No sleeping to recover magicka, No skill books, and no ingredients from named town containers.
  • Naked start is now Easy. The Strength and Intelligence caps and the level 20 cap are Hard instead of Grind.
  • The Fighters, Mages and Thieves Guilds are separate major objectives. Dropped becoming Hortator of all three Houses and completing every quest in one town.
  • The Tamriel Rebuilt guild, Legion, Temple and Imperial Cult major objectives are now to complete all their quests in five mainland cities. Dropped the Telvanni Isles questline.
  • Removed eleven minor objectives, among them leaving skill books unread, talking to every named NPC in Pelagiad and finding your way out of Vivec's sewers.
  • Starting spells lists what a new character begins with: spells from the race and the birthsign, and the starter spells the game gives for strong enough magic skills.
  • Argonian Water Breathing and Khajiit Eye of Night are listed as spells, not a constant effect and a power, and so are The Lord's Blood of the North and The Serpent's Star-Curse.
  • Starting sheet: skills sit in an even grid, and Health, Fatigue and Magicka share a row.
  • The custom builder's fields sit in two columns.
  • Optimize gear no longer shows a “Full kit by slot” heading.
  • Removed Copy this loadout, Save build and build codes. The address bar keeps a link to the current sheet, and Copy link copies it.
  • About and Changelog open from the links at the bottom of the page. About has separate Corrections and Support sections, with Ko-fi and PayPal, and says restrictions and objectives are curated by hand.
  • Fixed the navigation overlapping the site name in desktop windows narrower than 1200 pixels.
  • The page frame and cards have the game's rough parchment window border, and buttons and dropdowns look like its Name, Race, Class and Sign buttons, drawn pixel for pixel in the colours of its character sheet.
  • The starting sheet shows Health, Magicka and Fatigue as red, blue and green bars, like the game's character sheet.

September 14, 2026

  • Places in gear locations and objectives now show their region, read from the game data.
  • The early-game note now says what Mentor's Ring is and where it lies.
  • ARCE races and classes now come from the ARCE 4.1 plugin and Tamriel_Data: 21 races, including the seven Khajiit furstocks, Naga, Ayleid, Ogre and Reachman, and 67 classes. Races ARCE doesn't add, such as Kamal and Tang Mo, are gone, and each ARCE race has a suggested sheet built from its skill bonuses.
  • Checked restrictions and objectives against the game data. Objectives that named the wrong person or place now match the game (Fargoth's hiding place, the Foreign Quarter's levels, Lake Fjalding), impossible ones are replaced (clearing a bounty by persuasion, finishing the main quest in no faction), and vague Tamriel Rebuilt major objectives are now real questlines.
  • Tamriel Rebuilt runs no longer roll “No Tribunal or Bloodmoon DLC”: Tamriel Rebuilt needs both.
  • The custom builder and the challenge-run character now share one sheet calculation, so a skill picked twice no longer counts twice on the challenge sheet, and both warn when a skill or favored attribute is picked twice.
  • Fixed the custom builder's skill pickers swapping in a skill from before a class preset.
  • Fixed the challenge-run skill pickers, which didn't swap a skill picked twice or update the sheet.
  • Gear tables list one item per row, with where to get it and a short note. Alternatives appear as "or" rows, and Tamriel Rebuilt picks are labelled.
  • Every clothing and jewelry pick now names a shop or place. The early caster kit no longer suggests the Bone Charm: it only turns up in Tel Uvirith's tower, which isn't built until the Telvanni stronghold quests.
  • Added this changelog and an email address for corrections.
  • Tamriel Rebuilt gear now comes from the Tamriel_Data 26.08 and TR_Mainland 26.08 plugins: each slot names a Tamriel Rebuilt item that beats the vanilla pick, with where to find it.
  • Gear tied to a quest now says which quest to finish first — for example, the Gauntlets of the Poor and the Narsis Mages Guild.
  • Early-game gear follows a stricter rule: no harder to get than Mentor's Ring — a ring in Samarys Ancestral Tomb near Seyda Neen — is for a new character. The early melee ring is now the Shadowweave Ring.
  • Corrected classes, race skill bonuses, birthsign powers, premade builds, enchantments and item locations against the game files.
  • Removed Tamriel Rebuilt objectives that couldn't be completed: joining House Dres on the mainland, and the King of Narsis. Passwall is now listed once, as the spell it is.
  • Labelled Tamriel Rebuilt content with its release, 26.08 Poison Song.

September 13, 2026

  • The gear optimizer suggests shields for your own armor class, keeps caster jewelry for casters, and no longer tells melee builds to wear Mentor's Ring.
  • Fixed skill and favored-attribute pickers keeping choices from a discarded sheet.
  • Corrected where to find Ten Pace Boots, the Cuirass of the Savior's Hide, the Helm of Oreyn Bearclaw, the Fist of Randagulf gauntlets, Mentor's Ring, Boots of the Apostle and Boots of Blinding Speed.
  • Fixed the desktop header squashing the site name.
  • First public version of the build planner and challenge run generator.
